How your information spreads

Data brokers collect and combine information from public records, commercial sources, and online activity. People-search sites can make personal details easy for strangers to find.

Scan. Request removal.

The privacy scan brings your details, identity confirmation, and removal authorization into one flow. Submitting authorizes both a supported-broker search and removal requests on your behalf.

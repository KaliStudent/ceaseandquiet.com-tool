    UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM

    What is a data broker?

    A business that collects information about people, combines it, and makes it available to other organizations or people.

    THE COLLECTION

    Small details become a profile.

    Names, past addresses, buying habits, public records, and online activity can be combined into a detailed picture. People-search sites are one type of broker; many others operate behind the scenes.

    THE ADVERTISING

    Your activity can follow you.

    Tracking across websites and apps helps businesses build audience categories and target ads. Information and inferences can move between companies you have never dealt with directly.

    THE CONSEQUENCES

    Exposure reaches beyond ads.

    Public people-search listings can expose contact details and relationships. That can make unwanted contact, scams, harassment, and stalking easier. Removal helps reduce exposure but cannot erase every copy everywhere.

    Read the FTC’s guidance on people-search sites and online tracking and advertising.
